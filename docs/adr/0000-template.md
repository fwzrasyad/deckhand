# NNNN - Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who agreed (name, role)
- **SRD references:** requirement IDs and SRD sections this record comes from

## Context

What forces are at play. State the problem in the terms the SRD uses, and quote
requirement IDs (FR-, SEC-, NFR-, PROTO-) where they exist. Record only what the
SRD records; do not add background the SRD does not contain.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, then the specifics: values, limits,
interfaces, file names. A reader must be able to check an implementation against
this section without reading the rest of the document.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what must now be enforced. Include
the tests or checks that hold the decision in place (for example a contract test,
a lint rule, or a CI gate). Note anything the decision deliberately forbids.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative, and why it was rejected. If the SRD does not evaluate an
alternative, write "Rationale not recorded in SRD" rather than inventing one.
